🗺️ Muscle Dictation Quick Map

Say it naturally — we'll find the muscle

  • "Traps" → trapezius (upper/mid/lower recognized in context)
  • "QL" → quadratus lumborum
  • "Pecs" → pectoralis major/minor
  • "Lats" → latissimus dorsi
  • "Levator" / "lev scap" → levator scapulae
  • "SCM" / "sternocleidomastoid"
  • "Rhomboids", "suboccipitals", "scalenes"
  • "Rotator cuff" → supraspinatus/infraspinatus/subscap/teres minor

Full-body coverage: spine, shoulder girdle, arm→hand, glutes/pelvis, thigh/knee, leg/foot, abdomen/core, jaw/TMJ.

Laterality shortcuts

Your engine understands all of these:

  • "left neck stiffness" → every cervical muscle gets (L)
  • "both traps" / "bilat" / "(B)" → bilateral tag
  • "right knee pain" → right-sided knee musculature expansion

Regional terms auto-expand: "worked cervical" pulls SCM, upper trap, levator, scalenes, suboccipitals & splenius — tagged [regional] so you can audit inference.

Section-routing words that work

  • S: "reports…", "pain 6/10", "since last week", "aggravated by…"
  • O: "palpation found…", "hypertonic", "trigger points", "ROM decreased"
  • A: "consistent with…", "likely myofascial strain"
  • P: "performed deep tissue…", "home care:", "next visit reassess"
  • Plain "worked/treated/massaged X" → routed to Objective automatically

📄 Client Home-Care Handouts (print-ready text)

Cervical / Neck

  • Chin tucks: 2×10 daily, slow hold 3s
  • Suboccipital release: tennis ball at skull base, 1–2 min/side
  • Doorway pec stretch: 30s × 3
  • Upper trap stretch: ear-to-shoulder, 30s/side

Lumbar / Low Back

  • Knees-to-chest: 30s × 3 morning + evening
  • Child's pose: 60s breathing focus
  • Standing hip hinge drill for lifting mechanics
  • Walk 10 min after prolonged sitting

Shoulder / Upper Back

  • Wall slides: 2×10 daily
  • Sleeper stretch: 30s/side (if cleared)
  • Band external rotations: 2×12 light
  • Micro-break posture reset every 45 min desk time

Hip / Lower Body

  • Piriformis figure-4 stretch: 30s/side
  • Calf wall stretch: knee straight then bent, 30s each
  • Plantar fascia roll: frozen bottle 2 min/foot
  • Hamstring doorway stretch: 30s/side

🎓 Audit-Proof Note Playbook

The S/O/A/P formula

  • S: patient's words + pain score + aggravators + timeline. Never your opinion here.
  • O: measurable findings — tone, ROM degrees, palpation results + treatment performed with minutes.
  • A: working impression linking findings to function ("limits overhead reach, driving tolerance").
  • P: techniques applied, client response, home care issued, reassessment plan.

Weak vs strong sentences

  • ❌ "Worked on neck." → ✅ "Performed trigger point release to left levator scapulae and deep tissue to upper traps bilaterally, 20 min."
  • ❌ "Feels better." → ✅ "Reports pain 6/10 → 3/10 post-treatment; cervical rotation improved ~15°."
  • ❌ No goals → ✅ "Goal: pain ≤3/10 and full pain-free rotation within 4 visits."

Red-flag screeners to document

  • Radiating pain w/ numbness or weakness → refer out note
  • Unexplained weight loss / night pain → physician clearance
  • Post-surgical regions → surgeon protocol on file
  • When in doubt, document why you treated what you treated.
