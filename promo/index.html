Questions

Straight answers.

Does an AI make up my notes?

No. A deterministic engine maps what you said to muscles and body regions and sorts it into S, O, A and P. You review the draft and sign it.

Where does my dictation audio go?

Nowhere. Voice dictation runs locally on your own device; the audio never leaves it.

Is it HIPAA certified?

We make no certification claim. What we offer is privacy-aligned recordkeeping with encrypted, locked notes: client details and note text are encrypted at rest, and a signed note locks with a tamper-evident hash, so corrections become amendments.

What do I get on Seedling?

Up to 15 clients and one therapist, at $0. Online booking, the client portal and the waitlist start on Professional.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancelling stops auto-renewal at the end of the current billing period.

I work with other therapists. What does that cost?

Premier covers multiple therapists for $39/mo, flat. There is no per-therapist charge.
