Questions, answered

 

Does an AI write my note?

No — muscle identification is fully deterministic (rule-based), so it never hallucinates anatomy. The engine structures what YOU said; you review and sign.

Will my dictation style work?

Yes. The engine understands natural speech like "worked the neck, both traps felt ropey" — no rigid template vocabulary required.

Is it HIPAA-friendly?

Notes are processed locally on your device/browser session. We never train models on your clinical content. Client names are optional fields.

What if I bill insurance?

Select a payer profile and every note gains CPT unit math (8-minute rule), ICD-10 anchors, medical-necessity language and an audit checklist.

Can I use it on my phone between sessions?

Yes — install it as an app from your browser (Add to Home Screen) and dictate right from the treatment room.

How does client management work?

Professional and Premier plans include comprehensive client profiles with medical history, goals, progress tracking, and automated communication tools to improve retention.

What integrations are available?

We integrate with major scheduling platforms, payment processors, and practice management systems. API access available on Premier plan.
