Professional questions, answered

 

How does the voice-to-text maintain clinical accuracy?

Our engine uses deterministic muscle mapping (not AI) to ensure anatomical precision. Every muscle identification is rule-based, eliminating hallucinations. Clinical terminology is processed through a specialized medical dictionary.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Notes are processed locally on your device. We never train models on your clinical content. All data transmission uses TLS 1.3 encryption. Optional PHI fields can be disabled for maximum privacy.

How does progress tracking work?

Professional and Enterprise plans include visual charts for pain scales, ROM measurements, and standardized outcome measures (DASH, Oswestry, etc.) with automated trend analysis and reporting.

Can I customize treatment templates?

Yes, Professional plan includes pre-built templates for common conditions. Enterprise adds custom template builder with evidence-based protocols and clinic-specific customization.

What mobile features are available?

Full offline functionality, native app installation, voice commands, biometric authentication, and multi-language support available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

How does the patient portal enhance engagement?

Secure client access to appointment history, home care plans, progress tracking, and secure messaging. Includes educational resources and outcome measure tracking with gamification elements.
