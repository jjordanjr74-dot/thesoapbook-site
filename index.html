Questions, answered

 

Does an AI write my note?

No — muscle identification is fully deterministic (rule-based), so it never hallucinates anatomy. The engine structures what YOU said; you review and sign.

Will my dictation style work?

Yes. The engine understands natural speech like "worked the neck, both traps felt ropey" — no rigid template vocabulary required.

Is it HIPAA-friendly?

Notes are processed locally on your device/browser session. We never train models on your clinical content. Client names are optional fields.

What if I bill insurance?

Select a payer profile and every note gains CPT unit math (8-minute rule), ICD-10 anchors, medical-necessity language and an audit checklist.

Can I use it on my phone between sessions?

Yes — install it as an app from your browser (Add to Home Screen) and dictate right from the treatment room.

How does progress tracking work?

Professional and Premier plans include visual charts for pain scales, ROM measurements, and standardized outcome measures to track client improvement over time.

Are there treatment plan templates?

Yes, Professional plan includes pre-built templates for common conditions. Premier adds customizable template builder and evidence-based protocols.

What mobile features are available?

Full offline functionality, native app installation, voice commands, and multi-language support available on Professional and Premier plans.

How does the patient portal work?

Secure client access to appointment history, home care plans, progress tracking, and secure messaging. Available on Premier plan.

What analytics are included?

Revenue tracking, client retention metrics, appointment rates, and performance dashboards. Advanced analytics available on Premier plan.

Is continuing education available?

Yes, Premier plan includes CEU partnerships, clinical updates, peer collaboration, and mentorship network access.
